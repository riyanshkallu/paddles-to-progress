# Paddles to Progress

Website for Paddles to Progress, a student-run pickleball club for ages 13 to 19 that runs open play, drills and charity tournaments. Tournament entry fees and donations go to our partner food pantry.

The whole site is one file, `index.html`, with no build step. Open it in a browser to see it.

## Sections

- Hero with an illustrated court and the main Join button
- Where the money goes (play, collect, give to the pantry)
- About the club
- Come play with us (open play and drills by skill level)
- Tournaments (next event, sample day schedule, bracket format)
- Sponsor a tournament, and our partner food pantry
- FAQ
- Closing call to action and footer

Every "Join the club" button links to the club interest form (Google Forms).

## Publish with GitHub Pages

1. In this repo, go to Settings, then Pages.
2. Under "Build and deployment", set Source to "Deploy from a branch".
3. Choose the `main` branch and the `/ (root)` folder, then Save.
4. After a minute the site is live at `https://<username>.github.io/paddles-to-progress/`.

To use a custom domain such as paddlestoprogress.org, buy the domain, add it under Settings, Pages, Custom domain, and follow GitHub's DNS instructions.
