Started by friends who couldn't stop playing

Paddles to Progress began in 2026 with a few high school students who spent every free afternoon on the pickleball courts. We wanted a way for more teens to play, and a reason for the games to matter beyond the score.

So we built a club. Anyone ages 13 to 19 can join, from first-timers to players who think they're the best on their block. We run open play and drills, and we run tournaments that raise money for people in our community who need food.

Everything is run by students: scheduling, brackets, sponsors and this website.

Come play with us

We meet weekly after school. We pick the days based on when most members are free, and post the schedule on Instagram.

New to pickleball

We'll teach you the serve, scoring and the kitchen rule in your first session. No experience needed.

Getting the hang of it

Drills on dinks, third-shot drops and staying out of the kitchen, then games with players at your level.

Ready to compete

Fast open play, doubles strategy and practice for our tournament brackets.

Bring court shoes and water. Don't have a paddle yet? Tell us when you join and we'll help you find one.

Tournaments

We host tournaments throughout the year. Each one is a full day of doubles, and the money raised goes to the pantry.

Questions

Do I need experience to join?

No. Lots of our members started with us. We split drills and games by skill level so everyone gets good rallies.

Can I join if I go to a different school?

Yes. Any student ages 13 to 19 can join the club and play in our tournaments.

What if I don't have a doubles partner?

Sign up alone and we'll pair you with someone at your level.

Do players under 18 need a parent's permission?

Yes. A parent or guardian signs a waiver before you play in a tournament.

Where does the money go?

To our partner food pantry. We post the total raised after every tournament.

Can adults play?

Our club and tournaments are for teens, but parents and friends are welcome to watch, cheer and volunteer.

Can I volunteer?

Yes. We need help with check-in, scorekeeping and setup at each tournament. Email us to sign up.
